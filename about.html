Objective

Career objective

Draft: rewrite in your own words

I want to build a career in power systems, where reliability is the whole point and the grid people depend on keeps standing.

A protective relay has to trip within a few cycles. A transmission line has to carry power through storms, heat waves and faults. I'm drawn to systems like these, which people depend on without ever having to think about them.

My internship at EP2 put me beside the relay panels utilities install in their substations, and showed me how much careful testing and documentation stands between a design and a grid that works. Courses in power system analysis, power planning and power electronics, and simulating converter systems in PLECS, gave me the theory behind what I saw on the test floor. Designing an integrated circuit in Cadence taught me to be just as careful at the transistor level.

After graduation I'm looking for a full-time role in power systems, protection or power electronics, where I can learn from experienced engineers and take real ownership of hardware. I'll sit for the FE exam this December and work toward my PE license. Over the long term I want to help build the more electric, more renewable grid that's coming.
