Objective

Career objective

Draft: rewrite in your own words

I want to build a career where reliability is the whole point: the protection that keeps a grid standing and the circuits that keep a chip switching.

A protective relay has to trip within a few cycles. A transistor has to switch billions of times without failing. I'm drawn to systems like these, which people depend on without ever having to think about them.

Right now that interest pulls me two ways. At EP2 I worked next to the relay panels utilities install in their substations, and I saw how much careful testing and documentation stands between a design and a grid that works. Designing a programmable integrated circuit in Cadence, from schematic to layout to DRC and LVS, showed me how much engineering fits into a very small space.

After graduation I'm looking for a full-time role in power systems, protection or IC design, where I can learn from experienced engineers and take real ownership of hardware. I'll sit for the FE exam this December and work toward my PE license. Over the long term I want to grow into the overlap between my two interests: the power electronics and control chips that a more electric, more renewable grid depends on.
