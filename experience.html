Quality Engineering Intern

Electrical Power Products (EP2) · Des Moines, Iowa

June 2026 – August 2026

My main project was writing the Functional Testing work instruction, the procedure QA testers follow to test the different relay panels EP2 builds before they ship.

Functional testing confirms that a finished panel actually does what its drawings say. The tester injects currents and voltages, then checks that the protective relays, communication equipment and wiring all respond correctly. Much of that knowledge lived with experienced testers. My job was to turn it into a standardized, step-by-step instruction that any tester can follow on any panel.

How I built it

  1. 01

    Follow

    Shadowed experienced testers through functional tests on real relay panels, start to finish.

  2. 02

    Ask

    Asked questions at every step: why this test, what counts as a pass, what usually goes wrong.

  3. 03

    Learn QA

    Learned how testing, QC and quality assurance connect, and where a work instruction fits in that chain.

  4. 04

    Perform

    Ran supervised functional tests myself so I could write each step from my own hands-on experience.

  5. 05

    Document & review

    Wrote the instruction in VKS and routed it through multi-level review across departments.

Senior Design · EE 4910 / 4920

Eddy Current Probes for Conductivity Measurements

Aug 2026 – May 2027 · In progress · Team of 6

Description

Our six-member team is designing a portable, eddy-current-based system for measuring the electrical conductivity of metals. An AC-driven coil induces circulating currents in the sample. Those currents produce their own magnetic field, which changes the coil's impedance, and that change tracks the material's conductivity without touching or damaging the part.

My role
  • Developing project requirements and evaluating design concepts against client and user needs.
  • Working through the engineering design process: requirements, stakeholder communication, concept generation and technical evaluation, toward a prototype.

Big-picture contribution

Eddy-current conductivity testing is how industries such as aerospace and manufacturing verify metals without cutting into them. It can confirm heat treatment, sort alloys and flag heat damage. A portable, accessible system brings that capability out of the lab and into the field, where inspections actually happen.

Project II · VLSI · Cadence Virtuoso

Multifunction Programmable Integrated Circuit

Apr 2026 – May 2026

Description

A four-function programmable circuit. A 2-bit mode select switches it between a non-inverting amplifier, an inverting amplifier, a 16-tap digital potentiometer and a DAC. I took it from transistor-level schematic through simulation and physical layout, then verified it with DRC and LVS.
